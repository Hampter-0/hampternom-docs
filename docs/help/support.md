# Support and FAQ

## Get help

- **Discord:** the fastest way. [Join our server](https://discord.gg/45gRX62b77) and ask in the support channel.
- **Email:** [support@hampternom.nl](mailto:support@hampternom.nl)

We help as fast as we can, but we're a small team and not online 24/7.

To help us help you, tell us:

- which plan or server it's about
- what you expected and what happened instead
- the last lines from your console, if it's about a bot

::: danger Never share your token or password
We never ask for your bot token or your password. If someone does, it's not us.
:::

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a custom bot or website cost?

Every project is different, so we give you one fixed price once we know what you need. You pay once for the build. See [Order a custom bot or website](/getting-started/custom-builds).

### Do I have to host my custom bot with you?

No. You can run it yourself, or add our hosting from €1 a month so we keep it online for you.

### Which languages can my bot use?

Node.js, Python, Java, Deno and Lua. Tell us in the order notes if you don't use Node.js. Need something else? Ask us.

### How long until my bot is online?

We set up every server by hand, within 24 to 48 hours after your payment has arrived. See [How hosting works](/getting-started/how-it-works).

### Am I tied to a contract?

No. You pay per month and can cancel whenever you want.

### Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. Ask us on Discord or by email and we move you to a bigger plan. Your files stay where they are.

### Do you offer refunds?

No. Digital services can't be refunded once they're active, as our [terms of service](https://hampternom.nl/legal#tos) say. Look at the plans and ready-made bots carefully before you buy.

### Can I run more than one bot on one plan?

Each plan is one server, made for one bot. For a second bot, order a second plan.

### Where are your servers?

In a European datacenter, on Intel processors with SSD storage, with DDoS protection.

### What happens to my files when I cancel?

We keep them for 14 days after your service ends, then they're deleted for good. Download a [backup](/panel/backups#download-a-backup) first if you want to keep them.
