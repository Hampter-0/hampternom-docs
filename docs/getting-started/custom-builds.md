# Order a custom bot or website

Building custom Discord bots and websites is what we do most. You tell us what you need, we give you one price, and we build it. Here's how that goes.

## What we build

- **Custom Discord bots.** Tickets, moderation, roles, games, or something no one has made yet. Built with [Discord.js](https://discord.js.org/).
- **Custom websites.** A site for your community, company or portfolio, designed for you and working on phones and tablets.

Want something right away instead? Have a look at our [ready-made bots](/bots/ready-made).

## 1. Tell us your idea

Contact us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/45gRX62b77) or by [email](mailto:support@hampternom.nl). The more you tell us, the better the price we can give you. Useful things to include:

- **What it should do.** For a bot: the commands and what happens when someone uses them. For a website: the pages and what visitors should be able to do.
- **Examples.** A bot or site you like, and what you would do differently.
- **Must-haves and nice-to-haves.** What it needs on day one, and what can wait.
- **When you need it.** If there's a deadline, tell us.

Don't worry if you don't know all of this yet. We'll ask questions and help you figure it out.

## 2. Get a price

Once we know what you need, we give you **one fixed price** for the whole build. You pay once, not per month.

There's no cost to ask. If the price doesn't work for you, you don't pay anything.

## 3. We build it

We keep you posted while we work on it and you can ask questions the whole way through. You get to test it before it goes live, so you can tell us what to change.

## 4. Launch

When it's done, you get the finished bot or website.

- **Want us to keep it online?** Add [bot hosting](https://hampternom.nl/bots#plans) from €1 a month. We run it for you on our servers and you manage it in [the panel](/panel/overview).
- **Prefer to run it yourself?** That's fine too. Our [self-hosting guides](/self-hosting/vps) show you how to set up your own server.

## Questions?

Ask us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/45gRX62b77). You can also see our ready-made bots in action there.
