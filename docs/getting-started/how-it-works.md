# How hosting works

From your order to a bot that runs 24/7, in four steps. Most people are online within two days.

::: info Want something built?
This page is about hosting. If you want us to build a bot or website for you, see [Order a custom bot or website](/getting-started/custom-builds).
:::

## 1. Pick what you need

- **Discord bot hosting.** You bring your own bot and we keep it running. Plans start at €1 a month. See the [plans](https://hampternom.nl/bots#plans).
- **Ready-made bots.** A Ticket Bot or Server Bot you can use without writing code, with hosting as an add-on. See [Ready-made bots](/bots/ready-made).
- **Website hosting.** Your website on your own domain, with free SSL, a database and email. Plans start at €2 a month. See the [plans](https://hampternom.nl/websites#plans) and [Connect your domain](/websites/connect-domain).

::: tip Not sure which plan you need?
Most small bots run fine on the 0.5 GB plan. Bots that play music, use a database or serve many servers usually need 1 GB or more. You can [upgrade later](/help/support#can-i-upgrade-my-plan-later) without losing anything.
:::

## 2. Order and pay

Order in the [client area](https://billing.hampternom.nl). You get an invoice, which you pay by bank transfer or Tikkie. See [Ordering and payment](/getting-started/ordering) for the details.

## 3. We set up your server

Once your payment has arrived, we set up your server by hand. This takes **24 to 48 hours**. We create it with the right language for your bot, so tell us in the order notes if you use something other than Node.js.

When it's ready, you get your login for the panel.

## 4. Manage it in the panel

The panel is where you upload your code, start and stop your bot, read its logs and make backups. Start with:

- [Your panel](/panel/overview) for a tour of the pages
- [Host your own bot](/bots/upload) to get your bot online

If you get stuck, [ask us on Discord](https://discord.gg/45gRX62b77).
