# Ordering and payment

You order and pay in the client area. That's also where you find your invoices and active services.

## Place an order

1. Go to the [client area](https://billing.hampternom.nl) and create an account, or log in.
2. Choose a plan and add it to your cart.
3. In the order notes, tell us which language your bot uses (Node.js, Python, Java, Deno or Lua) if it isn't Node.js.
4. Place the order. You get an invoice right away.

## Pay your invoice

We don't take card payments yet. You pay by bank transfer or Tikkie instead:

- **Bank transfer:** transfer the amount to the IBAN on your invoice.
- **Tikkie:** ask for a Tikkie on [Discord](https://discord.gg/45gRX62b77) or by [email](mailto:support@hampternom.nl).

::: warning Always use your invoice number
Put the invoice number in the description of your payment. Without it we can't tell which invoice you paid, and your order waits until we figure it out.
:::

We check payments by hand, so it can take up to a day before your invoice shows as paid. After that we set up your server within 24 to 48 hours.

## Monthly renewal

Hosting is paid per month. You get a new invoice before your next month starts. Pay it the same way, with the invoice number in the description.

If an invoice stays unpaid, your server is suspended until it's paid. Your files stay for 14 days after your service ends, then they're deleted for good, as described in our [terms of service](https://hampternom.nl/legal#tos).

## No contract

You're never tied to a contract. To stop, cancel the service in the client area, or tell us on Discord. Digital services can't be refunded once they're active, see the [terms of service](https://hampternom.nl/legal#tos).
